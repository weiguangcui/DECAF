Abstract: The AdS/CFT correspondence allows us to relate gravitational theories in AdS space to lower-dimensional conformal field theories. In familiar examples in string theory, however, the size of the extra dimensions is comparable to the AdS curvature radius. This raises the question of whether we can find holographic duals for scale-separated AdS vacua, where the extra dimensions are much smaller than the AdS radius. In this talk, we explore whether we can identify brane configurations and candidate dual CFTs for such vacua. We discuss some unusual features of the resulting geometries, particularly their asymptotic behavior, and focus on whether the brane degrees of freedom can decouple from the asymptotic bulk.

Speaker Bio: Fien Apers is a postdoctoral researcher at the Instituto de Física Teórica (IFT UAM-CSIC) in Madrid. She obtained her PhD from the University of Oxford. Her research focuses on string theory and quantum gravity, with particular interests in string compactifications, the Swampland program, and holography. Her recent work explores scale-separated AdS vacua and their possible holographic dual descriptions.
